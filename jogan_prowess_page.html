Jōgan

Name: Jōgan (浄眼, Pure Eye)

Debut: Boruto Episode 1

Users: Boruto Uzumaki

Classification: Dōjutsu

Status: Canon (Anime Origin)

The Jōgan is a mysterious dōjutsu that appears exclusively in Boruto Uzumaki. Though its full powers remain shrouded in mystery, it is considered a divine eye tied deeply to the Ōtsutsuki clan, fate, and dimensional balance. Designed with input from Masashi Kishimoto and confirmed by Mikio Ikemoto, the Jōgan represents a new lineage of ocular power meant to surpass all others.

Abilities

  • Karma Inversion: Nullifies or reverses enemy Karma seals and Ōtsutsuki links.
  • Temporal Clarity: See alternate futures and outcomes with multi-branch foresight.
  • Dimension Rewrite: Restructure or collapse entire dimensions and spiritual planes.
  • Chakra Negation Field: Emit a field that nullifies all chakra-based techniques.
  • Balance Judgement: Detects imbalance in chakra and purifies or erases the soul accordingly.
  • Memory Absorption Vision: Absorb target’s memories, skills, and experiences through eye contact.
  • Eye of Origin: See chakra DNA and unlock or recreate ancient or lost jutsu.
  • Light of Rebirth: Revive beings once per soul by reconstructing spirit and chakra network.
  • Omnishift: Adapt body and chakra to perfectly counter any opponent in real-time.
  • Jōgan Ascension Form – "Eye of Samsara": Final divine form capable of restoring balance or erasing gods.

Comparison with Other Dōjutsu

  • Sharingan: Prediction and copying – Jōgan adapts and nullifies chakra-based attacks.
  • Rinnegan: Dimensional travel – Jōgan rewrites reality and dimensions themselves.
  • Byakugan: Chakra sight – Jōgan perceives chakra intent, imbalances, and future paths.
  • Senrigan: Foresight – Jōgan includes multiversal branching outcomes and decision trees.
  • Tenseigan: Gravity control – Jōgan manipulates energy laws and divine balance.

Creator Comments

  • Ikemoto: "Jōgan is not a Byakugan or Tenseigan. It is a new dōjutsu unique to Boruto."
  • Kishimoto: Designed with his approval and tied closely to the Ōtsutsuki conflict.
  • Anime Staff: "The Jōgan is a light of hope and cosmic balance in Boruto's journey."
